# Activity 10 — Customize the Chatbot

## 1. How did the responses change compared to Activity 9?

In Activity 9, the chatbot provided general answers to user questions. In Activity 10, I gave the chatbot a specific role as an AI/ML tutor. Its responses became more focused on artificial intelligence and machine learning. It explained concepts in simpler language, provided examples, and responded like a teacher helping a student learn.

## 2. Could the same model behave like a completely different assistant just from the instructions you gave it?

Yes. The same language model can behave like different assistants depending on the instructions provided in the system prompt. For example, it can act as an AI/ML tutor, coding assistant, cybersecurity mentor, or customer support assistant. The instructions influence its tone, response style, and the type of help it provides, even though the underlying model remains the same.
